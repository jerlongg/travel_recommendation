Our story

We believe the world is worth wandering.

Wanderly is a small team of curious travellers, writers, and engineers building a kinder way to discover the planet — one recommendation at a time.

Who we are

A travel guide with a human heartbeat

Founded in 2021, Wanderly began as a shared notebook of favourite beaches, quiet temples, and underrated cities. Today it is a recommendation platform that helps thousands of travellers cut through the noise and find places that genuinely fit them.

We do not sell tours or take booking commissions. Every destination on Wanderly is chosen by our team for its character, culture, and sense of wonder — then written up in plain, honest language so you can decide for yourself.

Travel lightly

Stay curious

Keep it real
